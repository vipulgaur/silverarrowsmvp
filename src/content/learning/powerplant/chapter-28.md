## Explanations to Practice Systems Examination Paper

**9.** In a powered flight-control system the artificial-feel unit is shown in parallel with the controls, while an irreversible servo system operates the control surface. A capacitance fuel sensor responds to dielectric constant: aviation fuel is approximately 2.1, whereas water is much higher (around 80). A tank containing water may therefore drive the indication to full scale.

**12.** Refer also to the explanation of electrical-system principles in Electrics and Electronics.

**15.** Rapid cabin-pressure loss increases cabin altitude; the cabin vertical-speed instrument indicates a climb and cabin differential pressure decreases.

**16.** If the outflow valves close unexpectedly, cabin pressure rises and cabin altitude decreases. Differential pressure continues increasing until the maximum permitted value is reached, when the safety valves open to protect the structure.

**30.** Generator frequency depends on rotor speed and the number of pole pairs. At 6,000 rpm, speed is 100 revolutions per second. For 400 Hz, the rotor needs four pole pairs (eight poles):

$$ f = \frac{n}{60}\times p, \qquad 400=\frac{6{,}000}{60}\times p, \qquad \boxed{p=4\ \text{pole pairs}} $$

**34.** Use the same relation between generator speed, output frequency and pole-pair count as in Question 30.

**45.** Capacitive reactance decreases as frequency increases:

$$X_C=\frac{1}{2\pi fC}$$

With the voltage fixed, reduced reactance means higher circuit current. Therefore increasing the frequency in a series capacitive circuit increases current.

**55.** Engine performance is greatest when air density is high. Favourable conditions are **low air temperature, low humidity, and high atmospheric pressure**.

**59.** The power relation combines pressure, distance, area, cycle rate and effective rotational speed. Force equals pressure multiplied by area; work/torque depends on force and distance; power is the rate of doing this work.

**63.** Propeller **wash-out** means that the geometric blade angle reduces from the root toward the tip.

**66.** Bypass ratio compares the mass of air passing through the bypass duct with the air mass passing through the engine core.
